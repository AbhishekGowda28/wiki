# Think Like A Programmer

- Look for most constrained part of the problem.
  - As it would have most constrained figured out, it would be easier to solved and build from there.
  - In a problem where we are trying to assign different values to different variables to meet constaints, we should start with the variables that has the most constraints
    - To put it other way, the variable with lowset number of possible values.
  - Real world Example :
    - Choosing a restaurant to go out to eat, starting with a person with least desirable or minimum restrictions we can start with person who has most restrictions on the food they eat.
