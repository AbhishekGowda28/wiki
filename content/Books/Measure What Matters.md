> [!book-theme] OKR's - Objective and Key results.

Objectives are the what.
Key results are how. At the end of the cycle, we should look at key results are just say whether we completed the key results are not. It should be as simple as yes/no.

When defining the objectives, they have to be big and audacious. It should not be simple and can be achieve. It should be difficult to achieve but achievable.<br/>

**Traffic light method**<br/>
The key results status can be tracked by traffic light method. By assigning Green, Yellow & Red. This is to measure the status. Green indicating things are going smothely and will be completed within the time frame. Yellow means is some hindrance in the process but looking into it would help get to the finish line in time or on time.
Red indicates the KR is in danger on not completing.

Google follows traffic light method, Green (1.0-0.7), Yellow (0.4-0.7) and Red (0.1 - 0.4).

KR should be time bound and measurable.
