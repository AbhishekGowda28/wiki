> Getting back to ZERO

After wining the cricket world cup in 2011, then Indian team went on to loose many matches. This was because the team had reached the summit, but didn't know to descend from where they were and getting back to playing there cricket. Paddy gives example of Rafel Nadal, where he is seen in the guy at 4 in the morning, whether he had lost the pervious days game or won the game or had parted the previous night. He shows up and starts working. That is his ritual on getting back to ZERO.

**How can I reflect on this** - At end of the day, journal everything that had happened +ve or -ve. Show gratitude to everyone and to [[@ನಾರಾಯಣ|@God]] on the days work. Before closing the journal, do a little planning for the next day, not a detailed planning but just highlight of the next day, that needs to be achieved. On the next day, the score is reset. Now we are starting fresh. Plan the day on how it has to go. Think what would an ideal day would look like. Plan accordingly.

> Imagination

Picture yourself doing the thing, before doing the thing. And feel the ideal state. Now think from the current position, what are the things/links that are missing to acheive the state I need to be in from the state I am from.
This gives clarity on things that I can be lacking, now I know what I am lacking and I can work on them. And achieve them and again, picture from then state, what are the things that will again be needed to achieve what I want.
