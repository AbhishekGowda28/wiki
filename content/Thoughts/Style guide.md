Having own style guide for everything is very helpful, this helps in reducing the decision that need everyday. Example, if I start associating everything in dark theme, this helps my brain perceive everything in dark theme and help in understanding and reading my world better.

---

Along with [[Markdown Tips]], it is helpful to follow style guide. Obsidian it self has published style guides[^1]. Along with this there are other style guides published from google[^2] and Microsoft[^3]. Following these guides can help in take better notes and write better documentation.
